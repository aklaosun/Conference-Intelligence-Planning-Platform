CCSE Conference Intelligence and Planning Platform

The CCSE Conference Intelligence and Planning Platform is a proof-of-concept web application developed for Kennesaw State University’s College of Computing and Software Engineering (CCSE). The platform is designed to replace the existing spreadsheet-based conference tracking process with a centralized system for discovering, evaluating, planning, and managing conferences.

The platform supports conference and yearly edition tracking, historical data preservation, AI-assisted conference discovery and information extraction, human verification of AI-generated data, conference search and filtering, deadline and cost tracking, reporting, and outcome documentation. The system supports the Center for Applied Computing (CAC) and the Center for Data Science and Analytics (CDSA).

Key Features

Centralized conference database
Conference and Conference Edition tracking
AI-assisted conference discovery and data extraction
Human review and verification of AI-generated information
Source and verification tracking
Search and filtering
Conference planning and evaluation
Cost and deadline tracking
Reports and CSV export
Conference outcomes and follow-up tracking
Role-based access control
Historical conference data preservation
